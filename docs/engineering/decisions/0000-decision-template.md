# ADR NNNN: Short decision title

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded | deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Owner:** team or named role
- **Decision scope:** Android, iOS, shared Flutter, CI, or other bounded area

## Context

Describe the decision to be made, relevant constraints, source/reference evidence, and why an existing playbook rule is insufficient.

## Decision

State the durable choice in clear, testable language. Link the affected playbook chapters and implementation locations.

## Alternatives considered

List realistic alternatives and why each was not selected.

## Consequences

Describe benefits, trade-offs, migration work, compatibility concerns, and validation requirements.

## Rollback and review

State how the decision can be revised, its review trigger/date, and who owns that review.
